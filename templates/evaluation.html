16–17. Learning Curves & Validation Curves

18–20. Probability Calibration & Reliability Diagrams

A well-calibrated model's confidence (predicted probability) matches actual empirical frequency. The reliability diagram (calibration curve) visualizes this alignment. Sigmoid calibration corrects overconfidence in Random Forests.
